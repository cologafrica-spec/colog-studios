Become an Instructor for the CoLoG Residency

Help train the next generation of technical AI safety researchers in Africa.

CoLoG Africa is recruiting instructors and mentors for the CoLoG Residency. We are looking for machine learning practitioners, researchers and technical professionals who can guide fellows through an intensive technical curriculum and support their development as AI safety researchers. If you have experience in machine learning and are interested in helping develop technical AI safety talent, we would like to hear from you.

About the CoLoG Residency

The CoLoG Residency is a technical training programme designed to develop the skills and research capabilities needed to contribute to AI safety.

The programme will use the ARENA curriculum as a core learning resource, giving fellows practical exposure to machine learning, deep learning, transformer models and mechanistic interpretability.

Through structured instruction, coding exercises and mentorship, fellows will build the technical foundations needed to explore and contribute to AI safety research.

The role

As an instructor or mentor, you will support the delivery of the residency, helping fellows navigate the technical curriculum and develop practical machine learning skills.

Key responsibilities

—Facilitate technical learning sessions using the ARENA curriculum.
—Guide fellows through machine learning and deep learning concepts.
—Support coding exercises, practical assignments and technical projects.
—Help participants implement and understand concepts covered in the curriculum.
—Review assignments and provide technical feedback.
—Mentor fellows as they develop their interests in technical AI safety research.
—Support fellows in identifying research questions and developing independent projects.

Who we’re looking for

We welcome applications from people with practical experience in one or more of the following areas:

—Machine learning and deep learning.
—Python and PyTorch.
—Neural network architectures and training.
—Transformer models and large language models.
—Mechanistic interpretability.
—Representation learning.
—Technical AI safety research.

Preferred qualifications

—Strong practical experience in machine learning.
—Proficiency in Python and familiarity with PyTorch.
—Experience implementing and training neural networks.
—Previous experience teaching, mentoring or supporting technical learning is an advantage.
—Completion of the ARENA curriculum or equivalent practical experience is strongly preferred.

We particularly welcome applicants who have completed ARENA and can demonstrate familiarity with its technical exercises and learning materials.

Who can apply?

The opportunity is open to machine learning practitioners, researchers, graduate students and technical professionals from anywhere in the world.

We particularly encourage applications from African researchers and practitioners interested in advancing technical AI safety research on the continent.

Formal teaching experience is not essential. Strong technical knowledge and a willingness to support other learners are what matter most.

Why join us?

—Help develop the next generation of African AI safety researchers.
—Support fellows working through a rigorous technical curriculum.
—Contribute to building Africa’s technical AI safety research ecosystem.
—Collaborate with researchers and practitioners working towards safer AI systems.
