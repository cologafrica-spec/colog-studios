CoLoG Residencies

The hardest six months of a young African's life. A full-time, fully funded residency for people transitioning into AI and AI safety.

Choose your depth

Everyone builds the same foundations. From there you challenge yourself to go deep in one track.

Foundations
Machine learning fundamentals

Training, optimisation and the mathematics underneath them, built up until the abstractions stop being abstract.

Track
Interpretability

Open the model. Find the features, circuits and representations that explain what it is doing.

Track
Reinforcement learning

How systems learn from feedback, where that goes wrong, and what specification failures look like in practice.

Track
LLM evaluation

Design and run evaluations that hold up, including in languages the frontier labs have barely tested.

Track
AI security

Adversarial behaviour, misuse, model and infrastructure security, and the failure modes that arrive on deployment.

Who we look for

We are not looking for people who already do AI safety. Most residents will be new to it. We are looking for people who will challenge themselves to go quantitative, and who will burn the midnight oil to get there.

01Willingness to learn everything from scratch. No prior experience in the space is required.
02Evidence that you finish hard things without being managed.
03A reason you care about how these systems behave, stated plainly.
04Availability for six months, full time, with no split attention.

How selection works

Step one
Written application

Background, motivation and a short technical exercise.

Step two
Technical interview

A conversation about how you think, not a quiz on what you memorised.

Step three
Offer

A funded place in the cohort, with a stipend for the duration.
