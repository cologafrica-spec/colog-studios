Why build this here

The upside

AI and robotics could compress decades of development into years, in health, agriculture, manufacturing and public administration. Africa stands to gain more from that than anywhere.

The gap

There is global demand for skilled people to fill AI safety roles, and not enough of them. Africa has a deep pool of resilient, hardworking talent that can fill those roles worldwide once it is given the training and the backing.

Our response

Train people to build at the frontier, and to build safely. Place them where the decisions are made. Keep them connected outward.
