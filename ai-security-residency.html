Week 0: prerequisites and interview

Applicants complete these two courses before the programme. The selection interview is based on them.

—Harvard CS50’s Introduction to Cybersecurity
—Harvard CS50’s Introduction to Programming with Python

Interview (about 20 minutes): a short conversation. Applicants explain a few ideas from each course in their own words, write or read a small piece of Python, and say why they want to join and whether they can give the time. A place is offered if they show they understand the basics of both courses and can commit the time.

The readiness gate

At the end of Week 3, each fellow has a 10-minute live assessment with the lead instructor. The instructor asks about 5 questions picked at random from a bank covering Weeks 0 to 3, and fellows answer in their own words.

You need to pass it to start Week 4. A fellow who does not pass gets one retake the following week, and a fellow who does not pass the retake joins the next cohort.

Teaching and mentoring

The lead instructor teaches a live session of about 2 hours each week in Weeks 1 to 10, on that week’s topics.

There are five mentors, one for each group of four fellows. They join when the core starts in Week 4, and each mentor meets their group every two weeks for an hour, goes through a practical exercise with them and helps them answer their questions.

Fellows log their daily tasks as Not started, Complete or Blocked in the activity tracker.
