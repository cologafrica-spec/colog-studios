Who we look for

Fellows come from the residency, from universities, and from careers that look nothing like research. What they share is the appetite to finish something publishable.

01A question you actually want to answer, described in a paragraph.
02Enough grounding in your chosen track to start work in week one.
03Willingness to be supervised closely and corrected often.
04Capacity to give the fellowship the hours it needs.

How selection works
