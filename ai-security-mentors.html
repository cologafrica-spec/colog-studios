Mentor fellows in the AI Security Residency

Guide one group of four fellows through practical exercises and help them answer their questions. Mentors join in Week 4 and finish after Week 10.

The role

There are five mentors, one for each group of four fellows. Mentors join when the core starts in Week 4 and finish after Week 10. Every two weeks you meet your group by video call for an hour, go through a practical exercise with them and help them answer their questions.

The commitment is 2 hours a month, about 4 hours in total.

The four meetings

MeetingWeekPractical exercise
1Week 4AISB 1.1 LLM internals exercises
2Week 6AISB 2.1 coding agent exercises
3Week 8AISB 4.1 model editing exercises
4Week 10AISB 7.1 threat modeling exercises, and the capstone plan

Requirements

—Comfortable with Python and PyTorch basics.
—Some experience with adversarial ML or LLM security, for example some AISB sections or equivalent work.
—Can attend a fixed one-hour meeting every two weeks during the core.

What you do not do

—Write fellows’ code or give answers outright. Ask guiding questions instead.
—Run offensive exercises outside the course environments.

Support and recognition

Technical blockers you cannot solve go to the lead instructor.

Recognition for mentors (stipend, certificate): to be confirmed.

Apply to be a mentor

Interested in mentoring? Write to info@colog.africa and tell us about your Python, PyTorch and AI security experience.
