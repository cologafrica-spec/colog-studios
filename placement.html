Where people go

We work with each person on the route that fits them, not the one that looks best in a report.

Route
Global fellowships

The established international programmes that take people to the frontier. We help candidates get in, not just apply.

Route
Labs and research groups

Technical safety roles, research engineering, and academic groups working on the same questions.

Route
Companies

Teams building and deploying AI products who need someone who understands how these systems fail.

Route
Ministries and regulators

National AI offices and regulators writing the rules, who need technical people in the room.

Route
Founding something

Some people leave to start the organisation that should exist. We help them find the first backers.

How we support it

One
Direction

An honest conversation about what you are actually good at and which route suits it.

Two
Preparation

Applications, technical interviews and research proposals, practised until they are strong.

Three
Introduction

Warm introductions to the organisations we know, with a real account of your work.

Four
After

You stay in the network. Most people need a second placement eventually, not just a first.

For partners

If you are hiring for safety work, evaluations, research engineering or technical policy, we can put candidates in front of you who have already been trained and tested.

01Tell us the role, the level and what competence looks like to you.
02We shortlist from residents, fellows and alumni, with their real work attached.
03You run your own process. We charge nothing.
