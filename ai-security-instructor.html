Teach the AI Security Residency

We are recruiting a lead instructor for a 12-week, AI security programme of about 20 fellows. Content comes from the ARENA prerequisites and the AISB (AI Security Bootcamp) curriculum.

Apply to be the Lead Instructor

Your role

One
Teach the topics

Each week in Weeks 1 to 10, give a live session of about 2 hours on that week’s topics: explain the concepts, demonstrate and answer questions. Fellows study the resources on their own and the mentors go through practical exercises with them.

Two
Co-run the readiness gate

At the end of Week 3, join a 10-minute live assessment with each fellow, asking about 5 random questions covering Weeks 0 to 3. This helps decide who progresses to Week 4.

Three
Answer technical questions

Help answer the technical questions from fellows.

Four
Lead the capstone

Review capstone projects.

Who we are looking for

Must have

—Experience teaching or running workshops, courses or training programmes.
—Able to explain technical ideas clearly to beginners.
—Strong Python and PyTorch skills.
—Hands-on experience in cybersecurity and AI security or ML safety, such as adversarial ML or LLM security, at the level of the AISB curriculum.
—Good judgement on the ethics of offensive security techniques.
—Available for a weekly live session in Weeks 1–10, and for the readiness gate at the end of Week 3.

Helpful

—Familiarity with the ARENA and AISB materials.
—Experience running a cohort-based programme.

Not required

—A PhD or research publications.

What you get

—A cohort of about 20 beginner fellows, and five mentors to work with.
—The chance to shape how the programme is taught.
—Recognition for the lead instructor (payment, certificate, reference).

How to apply

Fill in and submit the application form. Shortlisted applicants will be asked to teach a short sample (about 10 minutes) on one of the topics above, followed by a conversation of about 20 minutes.
